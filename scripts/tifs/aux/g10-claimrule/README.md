## Definitions (short; full text in the output README)

- Rules: registered (shared pool, Satterthwaite df; one-seed cells transported); own variance (A3.1: s2_a / r + v_a, df r - 1); claim rule (registered and own agree, else 'rule-dependent' = INCONCLUSIVE; one-seed cells 'one-seed, descriptive'; structural nulls apart). Family-wise: Holm over p_max = max(p_registered, p_own) on F31 and F19, one-sided 0.025, no-transfer p_up@d, transfer p_pos@d; one-seed cells at p_registered and not counted (reading A) or at p = 1 (reading B).
- Model: the registered S1c harness, configs E / H / H05; Σ settings: r = 3 measured covariance (validated against S1c); exchangeable covariance at r = 3 (bridge) and r = 5 for every seed-pooled battery (all-r = 5, hypothetical); a15d = the A15-d data path (BERT AG News logit at r = 5 exchangeable, the others at r = 3 measured); the 31 cells simulated jointly, one-seed cells borrowing the same replicate's pool.
- Configurations: all0, alld (global boundary), one_at_d (no-transfer: cell j at d, others 0), one_at_d_T (transfer: cell j at d, others surely rejected), obs (true k = frozen k). Rates: MC SE binomial; expected claim counts: MC SE from the per-replicate sums of squares.
- Validation against aux/g4 S1c and the independent re-simulation: two-sided Fisher exact test (hypergeometric), pass p >= 6.33e-5.

## Key findings (same text as the output README)

1. **Validation.** The registered rule in this joint simulation (r = 3, measured covariance, 80,000 reps per config = 8 seed blocks) reproduces the registered aux/g4 S1c tables: 660 of 660 cell and battery rates pass the two-sided Fisher exact test (hypergeometric; pass p ≥ 6.33e-5; smallest p 0.0029, Qwen3 AG News logit AddSent p_equivalent_mu0@0.1, config E; max |z| 3.27; |z| > 1.96 in 0.027 of the comparisons). An independent vectorised re-simulation with separately written rule code and fresh seeds (settings H r = 3, E all-r = 5, H A15-d) agrees with every compared number (883 ok; 883 ok; 883 ok).
2. **Cell level, seed-pooled cells (16).** Under the measured heterogeneity (config H, r = 3) the registered shared pool covers BERT AG News EP 0.6732 and falsely calls it POSITIVE at k = δ with probability 0.1638 (S1c finding). The own-variance rule covers every seed-pooled cell at ≥ 0.9564 and the claim rule at ≥ 0.9568; under the homogeneous config E the registered minimum is 0.9524 and the claim rule's 0.9849 (conservative). Over every config, Σ setting (r = 3 measured and exchangeable, all-r = 5, A15-d) and δ the largest false POSITIVE at k = δ is 0.1661 (registered), 0.0254 (own), 0.0235 (claim rule); the largest false EQUIVALENT at k = δ 0.1630 / 0.0243 / 0.0232; the smallest coverage 0.6714 / 0.9511 / 0.9539. So the claim rule holds the nominal one-sided 0.025 and 95% coverage at the cell level in every simulated setting (within MC error), where the registered rule does not under config H/H05. The claim verdict equals the verdict of the wider of the two intervals (both share the centre k), checked in every replicate.
